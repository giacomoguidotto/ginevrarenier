# Ginevra Renier

Some people write love letters. I built a website.

This is a personal website I made as a gift for my girlfriend, Ginevra Renier, so she could have a place to share her artistic journey with the world. A home for her photography, her thoughts, and everything she creates — designed with the same care she puts into her art.

## What's inside

- **Home** — An animated landing page introducing Ginevra and showcasing her most recent work.
- **Vision** — A photography portfolio where she can publish and organize her projects into a browsable gallery, with a full-screen image viewer.
- **Reflections** — A journal/blog space for her to write about her experiences, inspirations, and creative process.
- **Essence** — An about page telling her story, achievements, and timeline of key moments.
- **Connect** — A contact page with a form for collaborations, commissions, exhibitions, and press inquiries.

The entire site is bilingual (English and Italian), and she can edit all content directly from the website through an admin mode — no code or deployments needed.
