# Ginevra Renier Studio

Some people write letters. I built a website. ❤️

This is a personal website I made as a gift for my girlfriend, so she could have a place to share her artistic journey with the world. A home for her photography, her thoughts, and everything I can keep up with, designed with the same care she puts into her art.

## ✨ What’s inside

- Her vision: a photography portfolio where she can share the way she sees the world with us, with a beautiful gallery.
- Her reflections: a blog space for her to write about her experiences throughout her incredible career.
- Her essence: an about page telling her story and achievements with a timeline of the key moments.
- A way to connect with her: a form for collaborations, commissions, exhibitions and press inquiries.

I wanted her to have a flawless and beautiful experience when customizing her portfolio. So I created an editor overlay just for her where she can edit almost everything on the website without writing code or re-deployments.
