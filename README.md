# Ginevra Renier | Photography

Some people write letters. I built a website.

This is a personal website I made as a gift for my girlfriend, so she could have a place to share her artistic journey with the world. A home for her photography, her thoughts, and everything I can keep up with, designed with the same care she puts into her art.

## ✨ What's inside

- Her _vision_: A photography portfolio where she can publish and organize her projects into a beautiful gallery.
- Her _reflections_: A blog space for her to write about whatever.
- Her _essence_: An about page telling her story, achievements, and timeline of key moments.
- A way to _connect_ with her: a form for collaborations, commissions, exhibitions and press inquiries.

I wanted for her to have a flawless and beautiful experience when customizing her portfolio. So I created an editor overlay just for her where she can edit almost everything in the website without writing code or re-deployments.
